# Privacy Policy for Stuffy

Stuffy does not collect, store, or share your personal data. There is no server, no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Your data

Everything you add to Stuffy (items, wears, wishlist, notes, photos) is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Only you can access it, and the developer cannot see it.

## Photos

Stuffy only reads a photo when you choose one to attach to an item. Photos stay in the app's own storage and are not uploaded anywhere other than your private iCloud.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Stuffy asks for permission to send a local notification. It is scheduled on your device and involves no external service.

## Exporting your data

You can export your inventory as a CSV file from Settings. Where the file goes is up to you.

## Children

Stuffy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
