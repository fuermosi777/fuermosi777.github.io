FAQ

How is cost per wear calculated?
Price divided by the number of wears. If you mark an item as sold, the resale price is subtracted from its price first.
What's the difference between Retire and Sold?
Retire archives an item you're done with. Sold also records what you got for it, which lowers its net cost.
How does the wishlist hold work?
Add something you want to buy and pick a 7, 14 or 30 day hold. When the hold ends you decide: Bought it, or Decline. Declined items count toward the money you saved by waiting.
Does my data sync between devices?
Yes, through your own iCloud account. Sign in to iCloud on each device and turn iCloud on for Stuffy. The developer can't see your data.
How do I get my data out?
Settings → Library → Export CSV.
I'm not getting the daily reminder.
Turn it on in Settings → Reminders, and make sure notifications are allowed for Stuffy in the iOS Settings app.
